Entangled Dimensions of AI Literacy

In the classroom, K-12. Click a dimension to pull its thread.

Each dimension has a question for students and an idea to try, for K-7 and 8-12.

Every idea here runs on a teacher's screen, so students don't need accounts. Most AI tools have age limits, so with younger students the teacher drives.

The dimensions aren't a checklist. Any one idea pulls on several at once, which is the point.
